Neighborhood Sampling Benchmark, version 1.0.0

This benchmark combines derived core-level counts and geometric support groups
from two public experimental resources. The benchmark tables were generated
by this study. Preserve the source citations below when using the tables.
The MIT LICENSE applies to the research software. Source records retain their
original attribution and licensing terms.

Colorectal CyCIF:
Lin et al. Multiplexed 3D atlas of state transitions and immune interaction in
colorectal cancer. Cell 186, 363-381.e19 (2023).
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2022.12.028
Primary release: https://github.com/labsyspharm/CRC_atlas_2022
Archived repository: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7554924
Cell tables: https://lin-2021-crc-atlas.s3.amazonaws.com/data/
The source repository distributes its software under the MIT license.

STARmap PLUS:
Zeng et al. Integrative in situ mapping of single-cell transcriptional states
and tissue histopathology in a mouse model of Alzheimer's disease.
Nature Neuroscience 26, 430-446 (2023).
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-022-01251-x
Data release: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7683099
The STARmap PLUS release is licensed under CC BY 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes in this benchmark: coordinates are scaled by median nearest-neighbor
spacing; cells are partitioned into fixed cores; three ordered neighborhood
outcomes and three abundance outcomes are aggregated. CRC marker-high gates
are fixed from two development specimens. STARmap labels are retained from
the source release. These are derived representations, not new experimental
observations. Source checksums and download URLs are in metadata/input_manifest.json.
